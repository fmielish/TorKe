## Jobs TorKe should not be the reason you attempt

Stop and use a qualified professional for any of these unless you are trained and equipped for them:

- Brakes, steering, suspension, airbags and other safety restraints, and anything a vehicle's safety depends on.
- Anything orange or high voltage on hybrids and electric vehicles.
- Gas lines and gas appliances, sealed refrigerant systems, and the inside of a main electrical panel.
- Garage door springs, microwave internals, structural work, and anything under a load you can't fully support.
- Anything you feel unsure or unsafe about. If you are tired, rushed, impaired, or working alone, don't.

TorKe's built-in safety notes are general reminders. They are not complete, they are not specific to your item, and they never replace the manual or common sense. A missing warning does **not** mean a job is safe.

## Reading trouble codes and other data

Codes, readings and recall information come from adapters, databases and websites that can be wrong, late or incomplete. TorKe only reads; it never clears codes or sends commands to a vehicle. A code is a clue to a system, not a diagnosis of a failed part.
